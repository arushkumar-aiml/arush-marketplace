The Problem

Freelance hiring is still manual, slow, and unstructured

Clients don't know how to scope a project. Freelancers waste hours on mismatched leads. Every existing marketplace makes humans do the AI's job.

For Clients

Writing a good brief is hard

Most clients don't know what a fair budget or timeline looks like, or how to describe scope clearly — so projects stall before work even begins.

For Freelancers

Discovery is inefficient

Freelancers spend hours writing proposals for briefs that turn out to be vague, underpriced, or a poor skills match.

How It Works
